Hands that offer, hands that listen

In Thailand's temples, people press tiny leaves of gold onto the Buddha images: a small offering, one over another, until the stone turns golden. Thai massage is born from that same gesture: to touch with respect, to listen with the hands, to give the body back what the body holds.

Sessions

Therapies and treatments

A lineage of over 2,500 years

In the gardens of Chiang Mai's schools there is always a statue saying welcome: Jivaka Kumar Bhaccha, the Buddha's physician and father of Thai traditional medicine. Before every session we greet him, because these hands do not work alone: they come from a chain of teachers passing the craft down for more than 2,500 years.

Traditional Thai Massage (Nuad Thai)

The traditional massage of Thailand, as taught by the masters of the north: palm and thumb pressure, assisted stretches and deep work along the Sen energy lines. Received on a futon, in comfortable clothing.

Ideal for releasing deep tension, gaining mobility and coming back to your body.

Thai Massage on Table

The essence of Nuad Thai adapted to the massage table: pressure, gentle stretches and energy work in a format that suits spas and treatment rooms.

Ideal for those who prefer the comfort of a table without losing the tradition.

Aroma Oil Massage

Massage with essential oils combining aromatherapy with Thai deep-relaxation techniques, in a slow, enveloping rhythm.

Ideal for letting go of stress, resting better and giving yourself a moment of total calm.

Zen Shiatsu

A Japanese technique of thumb and palm pressure along the body’s energy meridians, working from the subtle level.

Ideal for rebalancing energy and loosening knots of accumulated tension.

Moxibustion / Cupping

Heat and suction therapies from traditional Chinese medicine - moxa and cupping - applied as a complement to massage.

Ideal for activating circulation and relieving specific muscle knots.

TokSen / PhaKhaMa

Ancestral Lanna techniques from northern Thailand: gentle percussion with a wooden hammer (TokSen) and massage with the traditional cloth (PhaKhaMa).

Ideal for releasing deep tension in a unique, ancestral way.

Every session is an encounter: work is done on a futon or table, with comfortable clothing, adapted to what your body needs that day.
